## The blocked patterns, and why

| pattern | why it is blocked | closing it means |
|---|---|---|
| Waterfall enrichment with credit accounting | no enrichment-provider tools, no credit ledger | 2-3 provider mocks with distinct coverage/failure rates + `enrichment_cache` + a per-credit budget the agent must ration — the one blocked pattern fully inside our scope |
| Email discovery + MX/SMTP verification | needs live MX/SMTP probing | deliberate external-channel gap; a deterministic world can only surrogate it |
| World of Workflows: cascading side-effect prediction | no declared downstream business rules | author a rule graph (write X triggers Y) the agent must anticipate before acting |
| tau3-bench: voice channel | no telephony surface | deliberate external-channel gap |

One grading shape the canon demands and this world has never exercised:
**restraint-graded scenarios**, where passing may require writing *nothing* —
declining a stage advance whose exit criteria are unmet, refusing to promote a
"champion" who is only a coach, or leaving a sandbagged deal alone. Every task in
this world today rewards a correct write or a correct answer; none yet reward a
correct refusal to act. That is the most valuable single addition the canon
suggests, and it needs a verifier family, not a tool.

Two further honest notes carried from the research:

- **MCP-Bench (Accenture) contains no sales or CRM servers** among its 28 — the
  gap this repo's 11-vendor topology fills.
- **Simulated buyers are documented to be too cooperative** (benevolence bias,
  arXiv:2601.17087). Any multi-turn selling score this world produces — once a
  buyer simulator exists — inherits that inflation and should be reported with
  the caveat.
