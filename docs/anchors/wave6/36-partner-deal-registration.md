## Partner tiers

- **Select** (entry): signed partner agreement, at least 1 certified seller, and $250k+ partner-sourced TCV trailing 12 months (waived during the first 12 months of enrollment).
- **Premier**: $2M+ partner-sourced TCV trailing 12 months, at least 3 certified sellers including 1 certified on regulated products (13-product-catalog.md), and a current joint business plan. Premier benefits: named partner manager, MDF eligibility, and roadmap briefings.

Tier does not change margin — the margin table below applies identically to Select and Premier.

## Deal-registration form (required fields)

Partner name and tier; submitting partner rep (name, email); end-customer legal name and website domain; region (AMER/EMEA/APAC); product(s); estimated TCV (USD); expected close date; end-customer contact (name, title, email); engagement evidence (meeting date or correspondence reference); known competitor (Harborview Capital Systems, Atlas Prime Analytics, Crestline Financial Cloud, other, or none). Incomplete submissions are rejected within 2 business days with the missing fields listed.

## Conflict check and the 90-day window

- On submission, partner operations runs the duplicate match from 16-data-quality-rules.md: exact contact-email match OR fuzzy match on company + contact last name against CRM accounts, contacts, and open registrations.
- A conflict exists if (a) another approved registration covers the same end customer and product family within the past 90 days, (b) an open internal opportunity already exists on the account, or (c) the account is a named account under 15-territory-model.md — case (c) triggers co-sell review rather than automatic rejection.
- Conflicts are resolved by the regional Sales Manager within 5 business days; the earlier complete submission prevails absent contrary engagement evidence. A later duplicate registration is rejected as duplicate. The account-owning AE may not adjudicate registrations on their own accounts (mirrors the self-approval bar in 05-cpq-discount-policy.md).
- An approved registration is **protected for 90 days from approval**. One 30-day extension is available if a qualified opportunity exists and the Sales Manager approves. After expiry the deal reverts to unregistered margin.

## Margin table

| Registration status | Partner margin |
|---|---|
| Registered (approved, within 90-day window or extension) | 15% |
| Unregistered, expired, or rejected | 8% |

Margin is calculated on net first-year invoice value after any approved discount and is paid on collected invoices under standard Net 30 terms. Partner margin is separate from internal rep commissions; internal splits follow the comp plan (max 2 reps, min 20% each) and are unaffected by partner participation.

## Co-sell and named-account overlap

- When a registration lands on a named account, it converts to **co-sell**: the internal AE (e.g., Zoe Nakamura on Ironwood Group) retains opportunity ownership, the approved partner keeps the 15% registered margin, and a joint account plan is due within 10 business days.
- All quoting, discounting, and approvals run through the internal chain — Deal Desk -> Compliance -> Finance per 05-cpq-discount-policy.md, 06-deal-desk-charter.md, 07-compliance-review-checklist.md, and 08-finance-approval-thresholds.md. Partners may not paper named-account deals directly.
- Partner-sourced activity on co-sell deals is logged per 11-activity-logging-standards.md so forecast hygiene (12-forecast-methodology.md) reflects the true pipeline.

## Quarterly partner business review (QBR)

Every active partner receives a quarterly, 90-minute QBR: Premier reviews are led by the regional Sales Manager (Elena Vasquez for AMER), Select reviews by partner operations. Standing agenda: registered-pipeline coverage against the 3x target, registration approval and win rates (benchmark: 25% opportunity-to-win), certification status, margin and payment reconciliation, open conflicts, and next-quarter joint targets. Two consecutive missed QBRs, or trailing-12-month sourced TCV below the tier floor, triggers a tier review with any demotion effective the following quarter.

Example: a Premier partner registers Meridian Holdings (APAC, Treasury Settlement Suite, est. TCV $2.4M) on 01-Mar. The conflict check finds no open opportunity; the registration is approved 03-Mar and protected through 01-Jun at 15% margin. A competing Select partner's 15-Mar submission for the same deal is rejected as duplicate.
