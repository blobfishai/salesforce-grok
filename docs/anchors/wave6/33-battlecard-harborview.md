# 33 — Competitor Battlecard: Harborview Capital Systems (HCS)

**Owner:** Marcus Webb (Sales Manager) · **Refresh:** quarterly · **Scope:** all regions, Institutional Securities family

## 1. Snapshot

Harborview Capital Systems (HCS) is our most frequent competitor in Institutional Securities pursuits, appearing in roughly 40% of Prime Brokerage Onboarding opportunities. Primary collision products: Prime Brokerage Onboarding ($1.2M/yr list) and Treasury Settlement Suite ($2.4M/yr list) — see 13-product-catalog.md. Log every HCS mention as a competitor-tagged activity per 11-activity-logging-standards.md.

## 2. Their strengths — do not dismiss

- Deep prime brokerage feature set with a large hedge fund installed base in AMER.
- Aggressive pricing: typically quotes 15-20% below our list on onboarding.
- Fast, polished sales cycle and a strong demo team.

## 3. Their weaknesses — attack here

- **No ESG analytics.** No native capability and no contractual roadmap date. Our ESG Analytics Add-on ($180k/yr) runs in the same workflow.
- **99.5% settlement SLA vs our contractual 99.9%.** At 2,000 settlement instructions/day, 99.5% means ~10 failed settlements/day vs ~2 at 99.9% — a 5x difference in operational exceptions.
- Thin EMEA regulated footprint; client compliance reviews of HCS stall (recurring win/loss interview finding).
- Research content is resold from third parties, not integrated.

## 4. Landmine questions (plant in the 30-minute discovery)

1. "Ask HCS to put the settlement SLA and service-credit schedule in the MSA — is it 99.5% or 99.9%?"
2. "Which third party supplies your ESG screening, and is it in-workflow or a nightly export?"
3. "How many regulated EMEA go-lives did you complete in the last 12 months, and may we call two of them?"
4. "What is the remediation plan when settlement fails breach SLA two consecutive months?"

## 5. Objection-response pairs

1. **"HCS quoted 20% below your list."** Reframe to total cost: their SLA credits do not repay operational exception costs, and the ESG gap forces a second vendor. Price moves only within Section 6 authority.
2. **"99.5% vs 99.9% is a rounding error."** It is 5x the daily settlement fails at 2,000 instructions/day — ~10 vs ~2 — and each fail is an operations touch and a potential regulatory conversation.
3. **"HCS onboards faster."** Our activation follows the controlled gates in 09-order-activation-runbook.md that regulated clients require. Ask HCS which gates they skip to hit their timeline.
4. **"We already run HCS in APAC."** Propose a 90-day coexistence pilot of Treasury Settlement Suite with measured fail rates, and a migration path timed to their renewal window per 14-renewal-playbook.md.
5. **"HCS says ESG is on the roadmap."** Roadmap is not contract. ESG Analytics Add-on is generally available today at $180k/yr; ask HCS for a contractual delivery date with penalties.

## 6. Pricing-pressure guidance

- Never open with a discount; lead with the SLA delta and the ESG bundle.
- Authority per 05-cpq-discount-policy.md: Platinum 15%, Gold 10%, Silver 5%. AEs may not approve their own discounts.
- Above authority, or TCV > $5M, route to Deal Desk per 06-deal-desk-charter.md; TCV > $25M adds Finance sign-off per 08-finance-approval-thresholds.md. Approval order is strictly Deal Desk → Compliance → Finance (07-compliance-review-checklist.md).
- Prefer non-price concessions: multi-year term with the renewal uplift capped at the standard 7%, ESG Analytics Add-on bundled at list, phased activation.
- Do not price-match below the authority floor. If the deal is lost on price, record the loss with competitor = HCS per 11-activity-logging-standards.md.

## 7. Win/loss talking points

- **Win — Meridian Holdings (Platinum, AMER).** A 90-day parallel test measured 99.93% settlement on our rails vs 99.48% on HCS; the ESG bundle plus a 12% discount inside Platinum authority closed it.
- **Loss — Ironwood Group (Gold, APAC).** HCS won on price after we held the 10% Gold ceiling; the post-mortem showed Deal Desk engaged at day 40 of the cycle. Lesson: engage Deal Desk at the Negotiation gate per 04-opportunity-stage-gates.md, not after.
- Track the HCS-contested win rate against the 25% opportunity→win target. HCS-contested deals sit in Commit only when the stage and health conditions in 12-forecast-methodology.md hold.
